Getting started

WIWTv remembers every show you're watching, the next episode to watch, and where it's streaming – on your iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

The Welcome screen

The first time WIWTv opens it asks two things:

  • Your country – it's taken from your device; change it if it's wrong.
  • Your streaming services – tick the ones you pay for (Netflix, Stan, BBC iPlayer, Hulu …). WIWTv offers them first when you add a show.

Tap Start using WIWTv. You can change both later in Settings.

Then

  • Tap Add and find a show you're watching (see Adding a show).
  • Tell WIWTv where you're up to, and it shows the next episode in Up Next.
  • Tap the red ✕ beside an episode when you've watched it.

If WIWTv asks to send notifications, tap Allow for an alert when a new episode of a show you're up to date with comes out.

Up Next

WIWTv opens on Up Next – the "what shall we watch tonight?" screen. Each show has one row with the next episode and the service it's on.

Sections

  • Continue watching – shows you're part-way through, most recent first.
  • New for you – shows you'd caught up on that have a new episode.
  • Not started – added, but not begun.
  • Coming soon – you're up to date; the next episode's date is shown when it's known.

Ticking

  • Watched it? Tap the red ✕ on the right. The row moves on to the following episode.
  • Ticked by mistake? An Undo bar appears for a few seconds – tap Undo. Shaking the iPhone also works.
  • Tap a row to open the whole show.
  • Press and hold a row for Skip this episode or Pause show (hides it from Up Next without archiving it).

A show's page

Tap a show anywhere in WIWTv to open its page: what it's about, where you watch it, and every season and episode.

Episodes and seasons

  • Each episode has a marker: red ✕ not watched, green ✓ watched (and crossed out). A half-filled ◐ season marker means part-watched.
  • Tap an episode's ✕ to tick it; tap its ✓ to untick it.
  • Tap a season's marker to tick or untick the whole season.
  • Next up marks the episode to watch next. Greyed episodes haven't aired yet and show their date.
  • Tap an episode's name for its details and Mark watched up to here – handy when you start using WIWTv part-way through a show.

Watching

▶ Open Stan (or Netflix, Hulu …) opens that service's app, usually at its home page. The episode to look for is written underneath. Come back and tick it when you've watched it.

Your rating and note

  • Tap a star to rate the show out of five; tap the same star again to clear it.
  • Type a note – "Watch season 2 together", "Recommended by Sam". It saves as you type; tap Done above the keyboard to finish.
  • Ratings and notes are yours alone, even on a shared Household show.

Further down

Change service provider, Move to Just Me (or Household), Archive and Delete.

Adding a show

  • Tap Add and type the show's name. Results appear as you type; the year helps when several shows share a name.
  • Tap the show. Choose where you watch it – your own services are listed first. If it's somewhere else, tap Another service… – or choose Other / not streaming to type a name.
  • Choose Household or Just Me (see Household and Just Me).
  • Say where you're up to: Not started, Finished, or Up to… to pick the season and episode you've reached.
  • Tap Add.

WIWTv downloads every season and episode and keeps them up to date: new episodes and air dates appear on their own.

The free version follows up to 5 shows at a time – see WIWTv Pro.

My Shows

My Shows lists all your shows. The buttons at the top show those you're Watching, Not started or have Finished.

  • Search finds a show by name, service or even an episode title.
  • The ⇅ button sorts A–Z, By service, Recently watched or Recently added, and shows All or Archived shows too.
  • Swipe a show left to Archive or Delete it; swipe right to tick its next episode.
  • Press and hold a show for more: open the streaming app, change service, move it between Household and Just Me.

Archive or delete?

  • Archive puts a show away but keeps every tick. Find it again under ⇅ ▸ Archived or Settings ▸ Archived shows, and swipe left to Restore it.
  • Delete removes the show and its ticks from all your devices. WIWTv asks first.

Household and Just Me

Every show is in one of two lists:

  • Household – shared with everyone in your household. When anyone ticks an episode, it's ticked for everyone.
  • Just Me – only you can see it, on your own devices. Marked JUST ME in My Shows.

To move a show, open it and tap Move to Just Me (or Move to Household). Its ticks go with it.

Sharing your household

  • Go to Settings ▸ Household ▸ Invite… and send the invitation, for example in Messages. Sharing your household needs WIWTv Pro.
  • Each person uses their own Apple Account and needs iCloud turned on.
  • To stop sharing with someone, tap Manage sharing.

Joining a household

  • Tap the invitation link and choose Open or Join. After a few seconds the shared shows appear. Joining is free.
  • Settings ▸ Household shows who shares it. Leave household removes the shared list from your devices; your Just Me shows stay.

Ratings and notes stay personal, even on Household shows.

The Up Next widget

See your next episode on the Home Screen, and tick it without opening WIWTv.

Add it

  • Touch and hold an empty part of the Home Screen until the icons jiggle.
  • Tap Edit ▸ Add Widget, search for WIWTv and choose Up Next.
  • Swipe to choose small (one show) or medium (three shows), then tap Add Widget and Done.

Use it

  • Tap the red ✕ beside a show when you've watched its episode. The widget moves on to the next one.
  • Tap anywhere else to open WIWTv at that show.

Lock Screen

Touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customise ▸ Lock Screen, tap the space under the clock and add WIWTv Up Next.

A tick made on the widget reaches your other devices and your household the next time WIWTv opens.

Siri and Shortcuts

The first time WIWTv opens it asks to use Siri – tap OK. Then try:

  • "What's next in WIWTv?" – Siri reads out the next episodes to watch.
  • "What's next on Slow Horses in WIWTv?" – that show's next episode and service, or when the next one airs.
  • "Mark Slow Horses watched in WIWTv" – ticks the next episode and tells you the one after.
  • "Open Slow Horses in WIWTv" – opens the app at that show.

If Siri doesn't catch "WIWTv", say "What I'm Watching" instead.

The same actions are in the Shortcuts app under WIWTv, so you can combine them with others.

If Siri says WIWTv isn't allowed, turn it on in the Settings app ▸ Apps ▸ WIWTv ▸ Siri.

New episode alerts

WIWTv can send an alert at 6 pm on the day a new episode comes out, for shows you're up to date with.

  • Turn alerts on or off in Settings ▸ Notifications ▸ New episode alerts. The setting is for this device only.
  • If WIWTv says notifications are off, allow them in the Settings app ▸ Apps ▸ WIWTv ▸ Notifications.

Air dates come from TVmaze and usually follow the show's home country, so an episode may reach your service a day later.

WIWTv on Apple TV

Find WIWTv in the Apple TV's App Store (it's included with your purchase). Use the same Apple Account as your iPhone, with iCloud on, and your shows appear on their own.

  • Up Next shows rows of posters. Select one to open the show; hold the remote's select button on a poster for Mark … watched.
  • On a show's page, select Mark S04E07 watched, or select any episode to tick or untick it. Select a season's heading to show or hide its episodes; finished seasons start hidden.
  • To watch, open the streaming app from the Apple TV Home screen – the show's page tells you which. Come back and tick the episode when you've finished.
  • My Shows shows all your shows.

Adding shows and changing settings are done on your iPhone or iPad. Ticks made on the TV appear there too.

WIWTv on a Mac

WIWTv runs on Macs with Apple silicon. In the Mac App Store, search for WIWTv – it's listed under iPhone & iPad Apps – and install it. Sign in with the same Apple Account, with iCloud on, and your shows appear.

It works just like the iPad version. Use the trackpad or mouse to tick, and the keyboard shortcuts: ⌘N to add a show, ⌘1 to ⌘4 for Up Next, My Shows, Add and Settings, and ⌘Z to undo a tick. The same shortcuts work on an iPad with a keyboard.

Backup and moving devices

With iCloud on, your shows and ticks are kept in step on all your devices, and come back on their own on a new iPhone or iPad. Settings ▸ iCloud shows whether sync is on.

A backup file is still worth having. In Settings ▸ Data:

  • Export saves a backup to your backup folder (choose it the first time; iCloud Drive is a good place). The file name includes the device and date.
  • Save a copy to… saves a backup anywhere, such as OneDrive or Dropbox.
  • Export spreadsheet (CSV) – every show and tick, for Numbers or Excel.
  • Import backup… adds shows and ticks from a backup file. It never deletes anything, and isn't limited by the free version.

Refresh all shows now downloads every show's episodes again. Clear cache removes downloaded show details only; your shows and ticks stay.

WIWTv Pro

The free version of WIWTv does everything, for up to 5 shows at a time. WIWTv Pro is a one-off purchase – less than a coffee, yours for good.

What Pro adds

  • Unlimited shows.
  • Share your household with the people you watch with.
  • One purchase for your iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV – and your family, through Family Sharing.
  • No subscription, no ads, nothing collected.

What counts toward the 5

  • Shows in your list that aren't archived. Archiving a show frees a place.
  • Shows in a household someone else shares with you don't count.

Buying and restoring

  • Tap Upgrade to Pro in Settings ▸ WIWTv Pro, or Buy when the Pro page opens.
  • On a new device, Pro turns on by itself. If it doesn't, open the Pro page and tap Restore Purchases.
  • With Family Sharing, the people in your family group get Pro too.

Privacy

WIWTv has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

  • Your shows, ticks, ratings and notes are kept on your devices and in your own iCloud. The developer never sees them.
  • When you share your household, the shared list goes only to the people you invite, through Apple's iCloud sharing.
  • When you search for a show, or WIWTv checks for new episodes, the show's name or number is sent to TVmaze, which supplies the TV information.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. WIWTv only learns whether Pro is on.
