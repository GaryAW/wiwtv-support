Moving from TV Time?

TV Time closed in July 2026. If it was where you kept track of your shows, WIWTv can take over: it remembers every show you're watching, the next episode to watch and where it's streaming – on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

Get WIWTv on the App Store

Free for up to 5 shows. WIWTv Pro is a one-off A$5.99 for unlimited shows and household sharing – no subscription, no ads, ever.

Get back to where you were up to – in a few taps

You don't need your old history to start again. In WIWTv tap Add, search for a show, and under Where are you up to? choose Up to… and the last episode you watched – say Season 3, Episode 4. WIWTv ticks everything before it, and Up Next shows the next episode straight away. For shows you finished, choose Finished.

Have your list in a spreadsheet?

If you kept your shows in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets, or another app can export them, Settings ▸ Data ▸ Import from a spreadsheet… brings shows and watched episodes in at once. Save the file as CSV with a Show column, and Season and Episode columns for watched episodes. How importing works.

WIWTv can't read files from TV Time itself: TV Time never published its export format, and it closed before we could test with real exports.

What you get

  • Up Next – the next episode of every show you're watching, in one list. Tick it off and the next one is ready.
  • Where it's streaming – each show remembers its service, with a button to open it.
  • Coming up – your shows' new episodes over the next 7 days, at the right time for where you live.
  • New & returning – new series and seasons on your own streaming services.
  • Share with your household – one shared list for the people you watch with: when one of you ticks an episode, it's ticked for both (WIWTv Pro).
  • Share a show with a friend – they add it in one tap.
  • Your year in TV – your episodes, hours and favourite shows of the year in one picture to share.
  • On every Apple screen – iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV, with home-screen widgets.
  • Private – no account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking. Your list stays on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Questions

Does WIWTv track movies?

Not yet – WIWTv is for TV shows.

Is there an Android version?

No – WIWTv is for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.
