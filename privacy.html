Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026 · Updated 10 November 2026

WIWTv ("the app") is made by Gary Wilkinson, an individual developer in Australia. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

In short

The app collects no personal data. It has no account or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer never receives your list of shows.

Your shows and ticks

Your shows, the episodes you've ticked, your ratings and notes, and your settings are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, they are also kept in your own private iCloud storage (Apple CloudKit), so your devices stay in step. That storage belongs to your Apple Account; the developer cannot see or access it.

Household sharing

If you share your Household list, it is shared through Apple's iCloud sharing only with the people you invite. Your Just Me shows, ratings and notes are never shared.

TV information from TVmaze

To show seasons, episodes and air dates, the app asks TVmaze (tvmaze.com) for show information – for example the name you search for, or a show's number when checking for new episodes. Like any website, TVmaze receives your device's internet address with these requests. Nothing that identifies you or your list is sent. TVmaze's use of that information is covered by its own privacy policy.

Purchases

WIWTv Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; the app only learns whether Pro is on for your Apple Account.

Notifications, Siri and the widget

New episode alerts are scheduled on your device. Siri and Shortcuts requests are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The widget reads your list on your device.

Support emails

If you email support, your email address and message are used only to reply to you, and are not shared.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete any show in the app, turn off iCloud sync in the app's Settings, or delete the app's iCloud data in the Settings app ▸ your name ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Account Storage ▸ WIWTv.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with its date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: wiwtv.app@gmail.com.
