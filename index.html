Support

WIWTv remembers every show you're watching, the next episode to watch and where it's streaming – on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV.

Get WIWTv on the App Store

Free for up to 5 shows. WIWTv Pro is a one-off A$5.99 for unlimited shows and household sharing – no subscription.

Contact

Email wiwtv.app@gmail.com. The quickest way is Settings ▸ Help ▸ Contact support in the app: it adds the app version and your device model, so we can help sooner. Please don't send passwords or payment details.

Questions and answers

My shows aren't appearing on my other device

Check that both devices use the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and that Settings ▸ iCloud in WIWTv says sync is on. Changes usually arrive within a minute; opening WIWTv on each device helps.

How do I share with my household?

In WIWTv go to Settings ▸ Household ▸ Invite… and send the invitation. Each person uses their own Apple Account. Sharing your household needs WIWTv Pro; joining one is free. See Household and Just Me.

I bought WIWTv Pro but it isn't on

Open the Pro page (Settings ▸ WIWTv Pro) and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. With Family Sharing, Pro can take a few minutes to reach the rest of your family.

A show is missing an episode, or the date is wrong

WIWTv gets its TV information from TVmaze. Pull down on Up Next to check for updates now. If TVmaze itself is wrong, the show's page on tvmaze.com can be corrected by its community.

Why doesn't "Open Stan" go straight to the episode?

Most streaming services only let other apps open them at their home page. WIWTv shows the episode to look for under the button.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Delete shows in the app, or delete the app. Your list is also kept in your own iCloud: remove it in the Settings app ▸ your name ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Account Storage ▸ WIWTv ▸ Delete Data. WIWTv's developer never holds a copy.
